# Island of Copper: 12,000 Years of Cyprus

A free interactive history game about Cyprus, from the first seafarers around 10,000 BC to a future energy-hub chapter.

## ▶ [Play it now in your browser](https://para2222.github.io/island-of-copper/)

Works on computers, tablets and phones. On a phone, open the link and choose **Add to Home Screen** to install it; it then works offline.

**⬇ Offline copy for schools and low-internet places:** see [Releases](../../releases) and download the zip. Unzip it and open `index.html`.

## What you get
- **Thirteen eras**, each with a hands-on mini-game, and choices that carry into the next
- **Story mode**: a narrated picture book for younger readers
- **English, Greek and Turkish** (English has recorded narration; Greek and Turkish use the device's voice)
- **For teachers**: a printable teacher report, a lesson sheet for every era, "spot the anachronism" quizzes, three save slots so a class can share a device
- **Then-and-now sliders** comparing painted reconstructions with the same places today
- Settings for text size, easy-read font, reduced motion and volume

Ages 11 and up for the game; about 6 and up for Story mode.

## About the history
Each era shows what archaeology and records support. Where evidence is uncertain the game says so.
